Note Quest

A friendly word-guessing game on the beach. Every bottle that washes ashore hides a secret word. Type a guess and the letters light up to show which ones are right. Solve bottle after bottle as the words grow from 4 letters to 6, and see how many you can find before you run out of shells.

  • Word puzzle
  • Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced
  • Hints
  • Saves your best score
Play Note Quest

How to play

  1. Pick Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced, then type a real word and press Enter.
  2. After each guess the letters change colour. Green means the letter is in the right spot. Yellow means it's in the word but in a different spot. Grey means it isn't in the word.
  3. Solve in fewer guesses for more points, and solve bottles in a row for a streak bonus.
  4. Miss a word and you lose one of your 3 shells. Solve words in a row to win a shell back.
  5. Beginner has 4 and 5-letter words, 7 tries and 2 hints. Intermediate grows from 4 to 6 letters with 6 tries and 1 hint. Advanced has 5 and 6-letter words, 5 tries and no hints.

Controls

  • Tap the letters on the on-screen keyboard, or type on your computer keyboard.
  • Enter checks your guess and the delete key removes a letter.
  • Hint shows you one letter of the secret word for half points. Beginner gets 2 hints per bottle, Intermediate 1, Advanced none.

Tips

  • Start with a word that uses lots of different common letters, like a word with A, E and S in it.
  • Grey keys on the keyboard are letters you can skip.
  • A yellow letter is in the word, so try it in a new spot on your next guess.
  • Save your hint for when you're down to your last couple of tries.
