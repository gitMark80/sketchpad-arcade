What is Sketchpad Arcade?

Sketchpad Arcade is a collection of 53 free, original browser games hand-drawn in pencil like the back pages of a notebook, made by Restless Faith Media LLC, a small independent studio in Pensacola, Florida. Everything here is built to be played in a browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install and no account to make. Your scores and progress are saved on your own device.

The games fall into 4 groups: Arcade, Puzzle, Versus, Word. Arcade games are the quick ones about timing and reflexes, puzzle games are about thinking a few moves ahead, and word games are about spelling, guessing and sorting. Each game has its own page with a short guide on how to play, the controls, a few tips and a note on how it was made, so you can see what a game is like before you press play.

Most of the games began as pencil-sketch twins of the painted games on our sister site, Beach Day Arcade, and the site has started adding games of its own: sudoku, minesweeper, code-breaking and two-player games you play with a friend by text. The first question about every game is whether we would keep playing it after the first five minutes, and every game stays clean: no gore, no gambling mechanics and no chat with strangers. You can read the full list on the About page, and see how a game gets from an idea to the New This Week row in how we choose games.
