How to record a reel
  1. Tap Load game. Play past the how-to screen so the game is ready to go.
  2. Tap Ready. The title card appears and everything else hides.
  3. Start your phone's screen recording from Control Center, then swipe back.
  4. Tap the title card. The game starts and your hook pops in. Play!
  5. The end card appears when time is up (or tap the Play free tag to end early). Stop recording.
  6. Post the video as a Reel. If Control Center shows in the first second, trim it in the Reels editor.
