How to play

  • Tap one of your pieces, then tap a highlighted square to move it diagonally forward.
  • Jump over the other player's piece into an empty square to capture it. If you can jump, you must, and keep jumping if you can.
  • Reach the far side and your piece is crowned. Crowned pieces can move backward too.
  • Win by capturing every piece or leaving the other player with no moves.
