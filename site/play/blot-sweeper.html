Blot Sweeper

Ink blots are hiding under the paper. Uncover every clean square without touching one.

How to play

  • Tap a square to uncover it. Your first tap is always safe.
  • A number tells you how many ink blots touch that square, including corners.
  • Think you've found a blot? Switch to Flag and tap it to mark it with a little flag (or press and hold).
  • Tap a number whose blots are all flagged to uncover everything around it at once.
  • Uncover every clean square to win. Touch a blot and the page is ruined!
