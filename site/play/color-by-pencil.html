How to play

  • 1Pick a color from the tray. Its matching numbers light up on the picture.
  • 2Tap a numbered square, or hold and drag across several, to color it in.
  • 3Fill every square to finish the picture. Stuck? The magnifier shows you one you missed.

On a keyboard, press 1 to 9 to switch colors. Your progress saves on this device.
