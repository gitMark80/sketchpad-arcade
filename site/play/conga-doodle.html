  • 1A conga line of doodle balls is rolling toward the ink bottle. Tap or click where you want to aim and Stubby the pencil shoots a ball.

  • 3When a gap closes with matching colors on both sides, they snap together for a combo. Tap Stubby to swap to the ball waiting behind.
  • 4Pop every ball to clear the page. Balls with a dashed ring slow, undo or blast the line. If it reaches the ink bottle, you lose a life.
