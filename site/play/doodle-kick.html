How to play

  • On your turn, drag one of your five players back like a pool cue and let go to flick it. Pull further for a harder kick.
  • Everything slides, bounces and stops, then it's the other side's turn. You can flick any of your players, and they can bounce off the back of the net.
  • Knock the ball into the far goal to score. Score in your own goal and the point goes to the other side. After a goal, the side that let it in kicks off.
  • First to three goals wins. The line-up changes each match. Aim at the side of the ball to bend it around a defender.
