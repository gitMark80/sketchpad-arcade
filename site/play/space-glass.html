How to play

  • 1Pick a pencil from the tray. Its matching numbers light up on the picture.
  • 2Tap a numbered square, or hold and drag across several, to color it in.
  • 3Squares are tiny, so tap + or pinch to zoom in, and use two fingers to move around. Stuck? The magnifier shows you one you missed.

Fill every square to finish the picture and add it to your space collection. On a keyboard, press 1 to 9 to switch colors and + or − to zoom. Your progress saves on this device.
