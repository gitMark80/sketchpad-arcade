Chibi Chib

  • 1Every chibi is a picture of tiny squares, and every gem starts on a square of the wrong colour. Tap a gem to pick it up along with its same-colour neighbours.
  • 2Each open square shows its colour with a little dot. Tap a square with the same dot to set the gems there, or tap the tray to stash them. You can also tap a colour in the tray, then a matching square.
  • 3The chibis are detailed, so pinch or use + to zoom in close. If the tray is full and nothing can be placed, the level is over.
