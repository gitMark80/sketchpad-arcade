How to play

  • 1Swipe left or right (or use the arrow keys) to change lanes.
  • 2The path turns! When you see the arrow sign and the rail, swipe that way to take the corner. Miss it and you run into the rail.
  • 3Swipe up, or press Up or Space, to jump over low things and gaps in the path. Swipe down, or press Down, to slide under branches, blankets and nets.
  • 4Tall walls block a whole lane: you can't jump them or slide under, so go around. Bump the side of something and you stumble; two stumbles in a row ends the run.
  • 5Paper plane: fly over everything. Magnet: pulls stars in. Bubble: saves you once. Run over an orange turbo pad (or grab a skateboard) and you zoom through anything for a while.
  • 6Collect stars across runs to unlock new runners. Each one has a little perk.

Press P or Esc to pause.
