How to play

  • 1The beach ball bounces by itself. Drag the lighthouse left or right to spin it. Arrow keys or A and D work too.
  • 2Turn a gap under the ball to drop through. Every disc you pass scores points, and more in a row score more.
  • 3Sandy discs are safe to bounce on. Purple sea urchins and sliding pink jellyfish pop your ball.
  • 4Fall through 3 discs without bouncing and your ball glows with a rainbow. The next disc it hits shatters, urchins and all!
  • 5Land in the splash pool at the bottom to clear the level. Each new lighthouse is taller and trickier.
